01 / THE CHALLENGE

Reading support should reach every learner.

Students learn at different speeds. Identifying individual Telugu reading needs can take time, especially when teachers have many students to support.

Aa

Word recognition

Some learners may need extra practice recognising Telugu words and reading connected text.

◷

Limited assessment time

Individual reading assessments can add to teachers’ existing classroom workload.

↗

Different learning needs

Students benefit from practice activities that respond to the areas where they need support.

02 / OUR PROPOSED SOLUTION

Meet AksharaAI.

An AI-assisted platform concept where a student reads an age-appropriate Telugu passage aloud. Speech recognition converts the audio into text, which can be compared with the original passage to generate reading insights and suggested practice.

  • Voice-based Telugu reading activity
  • Estimated reading accuracy and speed
  • Potentially difficult words highlighted for review
  • Personalised practice suggestions
  • Teacher-facing progress overview

07 / ABOUT AKSHARAAI

A student-led innovation concept.

AksharaAI is a proposed education-technology project focused on Telugu reading assessment and learning support.

అ

Project status: Idea stage

The platform is not yet operational. A functional prototype is planned. Its assessment outputs will need validation using teacher-reviewed samples. Speech-recognition errors will be treated separately from learner errors.

Privacy by design: Any future testing involving children will require appropriate parent/guardian and institutional consent, careful handling of recordings, and safeguards for student data. AksharaAI is an educational support concept, not a diagnostic tool.
